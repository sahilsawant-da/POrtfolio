Engagement Models

Three ways to access Fortune 500 finance rigour — structured to fit where your business actually is.

Project-Based Advisory

Defined scope, clear deliverables, fixed timeline. Ideal for cost audits, FP&A redesign, PMI advisory, or financial modelling projects. Start with a specific outcome in mind.

Monthly Retainer

Ongoing advisory partnership — regular reviews, MIS oversight, financial governance, and leadership support on a predictable monthly engagement. The preferred model for sustained financial improvement.
